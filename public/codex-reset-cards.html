When should you use a Codex reset card?

A saved Codex reset can bring a covered usage limit back to full. If your regular weekly refresh comes first, using the card after that refresh can give you more time with usable quota.

Check what the card covers and when it expires

Open Settings → Usage & billing in the desktop app. Look at the balance and next refresh time for each active limit. Then open the saved reset to see the limits it covers and the deadline to use it.

With a full banked reset, manual redemption requires at least one covered limit to have less than 80% remaining. A covered limit already at 100% keeps its schedule. If a reset restarts your weekly period, the revised refresh replaces the one you had scheduled. Check the new time in your account after redeeming a card. OpenAI’s guide to Codex resets.

Use the card on Sunday or wait for Monday?

Suppose on Sunday you have 20% of your weekly quota left. It normally refreshes on Monday at 09:00, and your card expires at 22:00 that evening. Assume the subscription stays active, the weekly limit is your only limit, and you can use the amounts shown by the end of Monday. In this example the card restarts the weekly period.

Waiting lets you spend the old 20%, the first 40% of the new week, and the full 100% balance available after redemption. That gives you a net gain of 40 percentage points over the 120% you could use without the card. Redeeming on Sunday restores 80 percentage points immediately and moves the next refresh past Monday night.

Calculate your own reset card timing

  1. Enter how much weekly quota you have left and when it next refreshes.
  2. If your account has a 5-hour limit, enter its remaining balance and next refresh time, if one is shown.
  3. Enter the deadline for each reset card and when your current subscription access ends. Select your renewal or plan change if applicable.
  4. Calculate the plan and compare the estimated quota with and without cards. Weekly quota restored is the amount returned when a card is used. Net gain over plan accounts for how the card changes later refreshes.
